UltimateFiles is a free and open source file manager for Android built around two panels.

• Two panels: swipe between them on a phone, or see both side by side in landscape and on tablets.
• Drag and drop items from one panel to the other and choose to copy or move.
• Interactive breadcrumb, folder filter, sorting, multi-selection with a contextual action bar and a docked paste bar.
• Long copies and moves run in the background with a notification showing the current file, progress and speed.
• Name conflicts: overwrite, skip or keep both, optionally for the whole batch.
• Internal storage and USB OTG / SD drives through the Storage Access Framework.
• Properties with exact size, permissions and MD5 / SHA-256 checksums.
• History of completed tasks.
• Light, dark and pure black AMOLED themes, plus Material You colors.

No ads, no analytics and no Google Play Services.
